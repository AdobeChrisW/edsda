The Challenge

Six stages.
Too much friction.

JCDecaux's campaign lifecycle is one of the most complex in media — spanning planning, booking, deployment, and reconciliation across hundreds of clients, formats, and markets. Today, each stage carries unnecessary cost.

📋

Pipeline fragmentation

Proposal creation is manual and disconnected from inventory and pricing intelligence. Sales teams spend capacity on admin rather than client strategy.

🔄

Negotiation rework loops

Contract and booking workflows involve multiple handoffs with limited version control — creating rework cycles that delay campaign activation and erode margin.

📡

Deployment disconnects

Creative asset management is separated from media scheduling. Last-minute changes cascade into operational firefighting and compliance risk.

📊

Reconciliation at scale

Post-campaign reconciliation is time-intensive and prone to error, impacting billing accuracy, client trust, and team bandwidth at month end.

👤

Client experience gaps

Agency and advertiser clients lack self-service visibility into campaign status, performance, and proof-of-play — generating inbound support demand.

🌐

No cross-market intelligence

UK insights don't flow to other markets. Every region solves the same problems independently, multiplying cost and preventing global programme scale.

Asset chaos at scale

Distributed asset stores Compliance risk Manual proof-of-play

Intelligent asset orchestration

Adobe Experience Manager Assets becomes the single source of truth for creative. Automated format conversion and distribution to screen networks eliminates manual effort. Proof-of-deployment data flows automatically into campaign records and client reporting.

Today

Month-end manual reconciliation burden

Automated reconciliation at scale

Workfront and integrated data flows automate the matching of actuals to bookings with exception-based alerting for discrepancies. Billing documentation is generated automatically. Finance teams shift from data entry to exception management — recovering days per month.
